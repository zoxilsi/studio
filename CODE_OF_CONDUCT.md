# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing
viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility,
and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, harassment, publishing others' private information, or other conduct
that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through
[GitHub](https://github.com/zoxilsi). All reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.
